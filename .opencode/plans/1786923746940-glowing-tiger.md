# Plan: Resolve Hardcoded Constants TODOs

## Background

The TODO at `src/main.rs:21-22` asks:
1. Is `PTY_SCROLLBACK_LEN` the same as `DEFAULT_MAX_LINES` in `terminal.rs`?
2. Can it be made user-configurable?

**Answers:**
- `PTY_SCROLLBACK_LEN` (2000) in `src/main.rs:23` is NOT the same as `DEFAULT_MAX_LINES` (2000) in `crates/term-wm-core/src/debug_log.rs:11`. The latter controls the debug log ring buffer, not terminal scrollback.
- `PTY_SCROLLBACK_LEN` IS semantically identical to `DEFAULT_SCROLLBACK_LEN` (2000) in `crates/term-wm-ui-components/src/terminal.rs:33` and `NEW_TERMINAL_SCROLLBACK` (2000) in `src/term_wm_app.rs:38` — all three are vt100 parser scrollback sizes.
- None are currently user-configurable.
